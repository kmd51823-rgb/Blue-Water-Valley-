Common questions

How is the purifier installed?

A technician installs it at your home for free after you book. We will confirm a time by phone.

How often do the filters need changing?

It depends on your water quality. The service team will advise you at installation.

Does it need electricity?

RO and UV models need a power socket. The gravity model does not.

Which type suits my water?

If your tap water TDS is above about 500 ppm, choose an RO model. Below that, UV or UF is usually enough. Book a free demo and we will test your water.
